Remote work has changed how many companies operate. A 2023 survey of 1,200 employees across three countries found that 62% preferred hybrid arrangements over full-time office work. People working from home at least three days a week saw their average commute time drop by 45 minutes a day.

The same survey also surfaced challenges. Managers reported that onboarding new hires was harder, and 38% of respondents said they felt less connected to their colleagues. These results vary by industry, seniority, and household situation. Some critics argue the benefits are overstated; others contend the problems are mostly a matter of poor management.

Companies that set clear expectations, invest in deliberate onboarding, and create regular in-person occasions tend to report better outcomes. Remote work isn't one model but a set of choices, and those choices matter.
